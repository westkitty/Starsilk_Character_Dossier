# Canon Moments — Forty Full-Size Illustrations

Forty separate full-size illustrations depicting the most incredible and
important moments from the Starsilk canon, generated using the canonical
character art in `docs/assets/media/` as direct visual references. Each moment
is cited to the chronology in `src/content/sections/chronology.body.html` and
the relevant character folios.

- **Images 01–10** — painted/cinematic rendering style.
- **Images 11–40** — unified **cel-shaded flat-animation style** (bold
  outlines, flat color planes, hard two-step shadows), matching the flat
  animation language named in the character visual locks.

All images are AI-generated illustrative material, not canonical media. They
depict canon events; they do not add canon.

## Character visual locks respected (all sets)

- **Shard-God Tiger** (01, 08): single long tail; digitigrade; obsidian body
  with luminous cyan-blue fissures and crystalline dorsal spines; cold cyan
  eyes; no cape; composed, deliberate cruelty — never berserk. Reference:
  `docs/assets/media/0cb9f2fd4623694ffca06f45.webp`.
- **Codec** (04, 10): bearded adult man, moderate capable build; black/dark
  field gear with red structure and asymmetrical chest identity; blue chest
  signal; **no cape** (the reference sheet's cape is noncanonical under the
  current lock and was excluded); blood-worn gravity, not spotless heroism.
  Reference: `docs/assets/media/eb35fd45ca321f41171c4c5d.webp`.
- **Dao** (09): integrated buster cannon and mechanical arm on the **right**
  arm, three-fingered hand; eyepatch over the **left** eye; gray hair; yellow
  jacket, black trousers. Reference:
  `docs/assets/media/e947e78027041e109d68912b.webp`.
- **Kail** (09): spiky blue hair; black fitted combat clothing; **red scarf
  physically covering mouth and throat** — no exposed speaking mouth; the
  throat anomaly reads as a gravitational distortion behind the scarf.
  Reference: `docs/assets/media/2ca43dd7199fb129f50e51d4.webp`.
- **Marcel** (09): olive skin, pronounced widow's peak, large round brown eyes;
  green sleeveless high-collar vest, cropped pants, leather wrist cuffs; plain
  spiral-tipped wooden staff — explicitly non-magical; his weapon is the
  wordstream, rendered as flowing glyph-script. Reference:
  `docs/assets/media/b7f4841c77f5a3f372c9ef77.webp`.
- **Drakken** (02, 05, 06): Egg, Lyriboris, and Cloudmaw follow their verified
  archival plates (`56dd8a1d…`, `b2270a96…`, `40c35dba…`). The Blood Ring (03)
  follows the Blood Rings folio's hard visual law: vitrified biological glass
  and wound-like scar material, never a clean Saturn ring.
