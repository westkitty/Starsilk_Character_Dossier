Core cosmology

  • The Notebook Program continuously renders the universe, its lore and physical law through memory and recursion; time travel is structurally impossible.
  • Starsilk is a literal programmable mnemonic filament running through stellar centers and the medium for repeatable reality Macros.
  • A successful star dive that pulls silk from a star becomes a star knife: the star immediately collapses toward a black hole and the system is destroyed.
  • The Starbinding released that rule across billions of successful star dives at once.
  • Death remains final. Stellar archives may preserve data, residue and structural memory without creating an ongoing conscious afterlife.

Moral architecture

The recurring conflict is not simply order against rebellion. It is the weaponization of necessity: who is allowed to decide what must be sacrificed, whether survival can excuse domination, and whether solidarity survives when consent is impossible.

Siege Wall visual law

A vast irregular swath of starless blackness. In physical space it is not a glowing fence, geometric grid, wireframe or visible lattice. Tactical interfaces may abstract containment; the sky itself remains a jagged absence.

Blood Rings

Drakken-made vitrified biospheric atrocity-structures: conquered populations and world biomass are rendered into crimson glass-state material, extruded into orbit, spin-shaped, cooled, and stabilized as propaganda, territorial seal, orbital shackle, recovery scar, and sometimes fortress substrate.

Beyond the old center

The Hookshot Network introduces a more material scale of travel: ships connect, latch, tension and move through infrastructure rather than behaving like independent magical teleporters. Route availability, tension, latching and ship preparedness remain material constraints.
