Drakken / Mother

Mother is a fixed living Drakken homeworld and hive-command ecology in the Aureal Nebula. Through the Notebook Program, Drakken Eggs and strains emerge under her continuance logic. Roughly two millennia after the Siege Wall, surviving Drakken defeat Mother in an anti-fascist coup and break the hive-mind condition as a governing order.

Administration

The governing order capable of genuine coordination and genuine atrocity. Its danger is procedural certainty: harm can become administratively unintelligible once the institution names itself necessary.

Wordstreamer

The Language-Bound Poet of the original Triumvirate and source lineage of wordstreaming. Tiger murders Wordstreamer with Starsilk in the primordial betrayal; the deletion produces the first recorded Starsilk bleed.

NiAlBu

The Recursive Dreamer, divine archivist and imprisoned witness to Wordstreamer’s murder. Tiger confines NiAlBu inside the Digital Geode; much later Codec kills NiAlBu at WorldsVault, deleting reality’s final deific checksum.

Jazen

Codec’s partner. At the Spire Event, Obsidian Armor consumes Jazen and his data flows into a nearby star, giving Codec direct evidence of the stellar archive and accelerating his break with Tiger’s system.

Starsilk

Tiger-made programmable mnemonic filament threaded through stellar centers, not a sentient character. Pulling it from a star executes catastrophic collapse; in the Partition it is rendered inherently inert.

Blood Rings

Drakken-made vitrified biospheric atrocity-structures. Gorevault renders and stores feedstock; Ringthroat extrudes and shapes it toward orbit.

Obsidian Armor / Torture

Autonomous punishment architecture powered by recursive suffering. Torture can suspend captured data across centuries; Dao and Marcel crack it at Zentrum to release Kail.

WorldsVault

Repository of thirty extinct-civilization planetary templates. Codec breaches it before Starbinding; the templates become the seed set for reconstructed worlds inside the Partition.

The Partition

Parallel reality forged from the data released by mass star collapse. Starsilk is inert there, excluding Tiger’s administrative code; reconstructed populations inherit template data without knowing they are reconstructions.

Hookshot Network

Connection-based travel infrastructure. Tension, latching, route availability and ship preparedness matter as much as destination.

Codified Waves

Wartime deployment doctrine built on the Hookshot Network’s blink gates — lines of ships latch, tension and move as a single unit across stabilized manifolds, letting the Administration land artillery and supply at stellar distance regardless of Drakken atmospheric rewrites underway on the ground.
