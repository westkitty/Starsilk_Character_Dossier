Visual manifestation

A towering, column-necked Drakken with a distended throat core glowing red-black beneath translucent armor. Immense stabilizer limbs anchor the body while the upper maw opens into a vertical extrusion funnel ringed by pressure rings and storm-lit barcode structures.

Blood Ring function

  • Lifts vitrification slurry through atmosphere in controlled streams.
  • Braids rendered biomass into orbital trajectories.
  • Applies compression to partial ring bands.
  • Maintains tension, pressure and spin alignment until early ring geometry stabilizes.
  • Turns processed feedstock into sky.

Combat profile

  • Offensive A: Extrusion Lance.
  • Defensive A: Pressure Column Shield.
  • Mobility F: Anchored Launch Stance.

Formation language

Red-black columns rise through storm layers, braided arterial streams cross the upper atmosphere, pressure rings force loose arcs into disciplined orbit, and partial rings close like a noose.

Lore fragment

“The world rose through its throat.”

The Closing of Fallenstar Prime: witnesses first mistook the red columns for stormlight. By the time the arcs joined in orbit, the planet had already been sentenced to memory.
