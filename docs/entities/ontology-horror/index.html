Starsilk / the medium

  • Literal programmable mnemonic filament and substrate for repeatable reality Macros.
  • Carries memory/data architecture including traces of the dead; it is not itself sentient or consciously suffering.
  • Pulling Starsilk from a star causes immediate gravitational collapse and destroys the star system.
  • Known safe interaction methods remain Tiger’s immunity, the Syrin blood glitch, and Codec’s Syrin-blood vapor method.
  • The phrase “stars don’t burn, they surrender” encodes Tiger’s reversal of Codec’s solidarity ideal.

Macros & wordstreaming

Macros are sacred/repeatable executable action loops embedded in reality: literal runtime commands, not metaphor. Wordstreaming is the dead ancient language used to weave reality-code before Tiger’s enclosure of the system; Marcel remains its major living practitioner.

Tiger Equation / meta-canon breach

The Tiger Equation first appeared unbidden on July 15, 2025. Within project meta-canon this marks Tiger’s breach across the fiction/authorial boundary, establishes the Equation as a sigil of overwrite and divine sorrow recursion, and places later narrative decisions in its shadow.

Administration knowledge asymmetry

The Administration can execute immense power without understanding Starsilk’s true origin or Tiger’s intended purpose for it. It is not simply an extension of Tiger’s private design; its ignorance allows bureaucratic violence to become a separate system with its own procedures and rationalizations.

Procedural sadism / conquest of meaning

Tiger’s cruelty is calm, surgical and repeatable. He does not merely destroy a subject; he revises the category through which the subject can understand what happened, often preserving witness long enough for the overwritten meaning to become legible.

The consent loop

In Fey-legal horror logic, wording binds more strongly than intention. A rebel can draw on the same executable syntax they oppose and thereby expose themselves to the runtime that syntax belongs to. Rebellion can therefore become legible to the enforcement system without becoming morally consensual.

Immunity paradox — unresolved hypothesis

The Syrin-blood vapor method unquestionably bypasses Starsilk protocols. A darker hypothesis remains open: because Tiger owns the underlying code-base, the “glitch” could overlap with an existing diagnostic cleanup path. This is not settled fact and remains explicitly unresolved.

Ghost-rituals & mnemonic anchors

People use physical checksums—vitrified stone, stitched notes and sub-vocal melodies mimicking dead-star data shimmer—to preserve memory against retroactive editing. These practices preserve records and identity traces; they do not resurrect conscious dead.

Fixed names / priority

NiAlBu is the canon divine name; superseded alternatives remain non-canon history. Syrin is the canon lineage name. Current confirmed terminology overrides older conflicting labels.

Naming law

Near the Shard-God’s direct influence, using the title the Shard-God is the structurally safer naming convention. Calling him Tiger can function as an act of resistance when distance permits it.

Fey boundary law

Unresolved syntax and orphaned execution threads accumulate at the extreme edges of rendering. Fey gifts can solve immediate problems while installing malicious long-term dependencies that bind the recipient to an external requirement.

Ration Authority

The Administration uses access to food and the requirement to ask for it as population-control architecture on fringe worlds: starvation converted into bureaucratic supplication.
