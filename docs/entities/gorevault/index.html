Gorevault

Dracoforma · Civiformer · biomass render crucible
Not an opening-wave siege beast. Gorevault appears when resistance has been broken and a world is no longer being fought over, but inventoried.

Visual manifestation

A colossal vault-backed Drakken with a swollen furnace body, pressure-sealed ribs and hanging reservoir sacs dark with internal motion. Forelimbs end in hooked extraction claws; the spine supports segmented storage chambers ringed with barcode locks and ash vents.

Blood Ring function

  • Harvests population mass and world biomass at planetary scale.
  • Separates blood residue, ocean fats, biosphere oils, bone ash and organic slurry.
  • Stabilizes and stores material before vitrification and orbital extrusion.
  • Turns annihilation into measured feedstock: the logistics layer that makes a Blood Ring scalable.

Combat profile

  • Offensive B: Crucible Maw Intake.
  • Defensive A: Pressure Vault Hull.
  • Mobility D: Processional Haul.

Site language

Gorevault-linked sites read as planetary abattoir fused with storage cathedral: black-red reservoirs, pressure vaults, ash settling fields, suspended bio-slurry chambers and crust-deep containment ribs.

Lore fragment

“It did not bury the dead. It balanced them.”

The Tally Fields of Rhem Vor: after the world fell, Gorevaults crossed the continents in silence and the seas receded into storage.
