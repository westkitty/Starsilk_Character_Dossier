A colossal vault-backed Drakken with a swollen furnace body, pressure-sealed ribs and hanging reservoir sacs dark with internal motion. Forelimbs end in hooked extraction claws; the spine supports segmented storage chambers ringed with barcode locks and ash vents.

- Harvests population mass and world biomass at planetary scale.
- Separates blood residue, ocean fats, biosphere oils, bone ash and organic slurry.
- Stabilizes and stores material before vitrification and orbital extrusion.
- Turns annihilation into measured feedstock: the logistics layer that makes a Blood Ring scalable.
