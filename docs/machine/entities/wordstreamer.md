### Canonical record
- Language-bound poet who gave the universe its script and the dead ancient language later used for wordstreaming.
- Her existence allowed language to formulate refusal; this made her a structural threat to Tiger’s administrative enclosure.
- Tiger murdered her with Starsilk, turning the medium’s betrayal architecture against the syntax deity herself.
- Her deletion is the foundational rupture behind the first recorded Starsilk bleed and the orphaned Notebook Program.
- Marcel learned wordstreaming from Wordstreamer herself before her death.
