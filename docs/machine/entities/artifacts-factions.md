## Published source content

### Physical artifacts
- Torture: engineered anomaly that intercepts the data of anything it kills and compresses that data into physical mass inside the blade.
- Obsidian Armor: autonomous, non-sentient enforcement mechanism tasked with systemic stabilization/data conservation; it consumes its wearer.
- Codec ’s handheld vaporizer: deploys the Syrin-blood glitch as vapor, clearing or bypassing executable terminal lines.
- The First Dirt: glass cylinder containing living stabilized black soil recovered from a Level 3 remnant ecology.
### Approved war-era macro scripts
- AG-1 / Anchor-Grid: stabilizes ground against Telluric shifts; misalignment can cause local shear and sinkholes.
- CBF-3 / Corpse-Burn: sterilizes growth vectors and ovary fields; failure mode is collateral conflagration and resource vaporization.
- FBA-7 / Fracture Bomb: targets load-bearing structures to initiate Godfall; bad timing can harden tissue and delay collapse.
- WSS-5 / Weather-Stab: cuts corridors through Aeric storms; produces vacuum pockets and high fuel costs.
- ENF-0 / Echo-Null: counters Umbral command hijacks; disorients friendlies and causes sensory fatigue.
- MBP-2 / Marrow-Bleach: denatures gestation nodes and ovaries; leaves toxic residuals and biosphere drift.
- GSE-4 / Gravity-Shear: localized micro-gravity stress; failure produces unpredictable landslides.
- SD-8 / Seismic Dowel: fissures Telluric plates via penetrators; can open unintended sink zones.
- VA-9 / Vitrify-Anchor: Pyric anneal used to seal biological structures; leaves toxic vapors and a permanent hazard.
- BFR-11 / Bioforge-Rend: tears bio-metal composites apart at their joints; active shards persist after the strike.
- NSM-6 / Null-Surge Matrix: disrupts spawn signaling and hatch cycles; carries sentient neurochemistry side-effects.
- CC-12 / Cleansing Cascade: combines CBF, MBP and VA to render a region unrecoverable; the failure mode is permanent environmental scarring.

### Institutions & factions
- Ledger Administration: bureaucratic enforcement layer of the Shard-God’s design.
- Archive Cells: ground-level groups using data looms to preserve memory and evidence.
- Solidarity Cells: insurgent mutual-defense networks operating with degraded Starsilk and local autonomy.
- Free Runners: civilians and syndicate operators navigating volatile frontier spaces.
- Tiger Cults: zealot ship-strippers who negotiate with no one, often leaving the terminal message: “Stars don’t burn, they surrender.”
### Material principle
In the black market, trauma can be the commodity even when utility is secondary.
### Allkept containment doctrine
The Administration response to Allkept is extermination through subtraction: destroy host minds so the entity cannot be remembered forward through consensus. The doctrine mirrors the setting’s recurring terror—solving a memory-borne threat by attacking the people carrying memory.
### Field valuation & authentication
- Inert Starsilk Thread (com-04): ~800 CR pristine, 250–350 CR degraded. Macro-resonance test: exposed to a low-level data packet, genuine thread absorbs the signal and its weight changes as it processes the memory; counterfeit nylon stays static or melts.
- Blood Ring Glass (com-05): ~1,500 CR pristine, 600–700 CR degraded. Polarization check: a UV laser traps in recursive loops inside the genuine material; volcanic obsidian just refracts cleanly or cracks.
- NiAlBu Witness Shards (com-06): ~2,500 CR pristine, ~1,000 CR degraded. Code-dream audio check: a true shard emits a non-repeating acoustic signature of weeping and ticking; a cleanly repeating loop marks a scrubbed Data Loom fake.
